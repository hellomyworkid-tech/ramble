Ramble

Say it once and it's kept. AI turns what you say into to-dos, ledger entries, bill and expiry reminders, and notes.

  • Say "pay rent 3,200 tomorrow at 3pm" and get a to-do, a bill and an expense.
  • Works fully offline: speech recognition and AI tidying can both run on your iPhone.
  • Your data stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. We have no servers.

Free and open source (MIT License): github.com/hellomyworkid-tech/Ramble
