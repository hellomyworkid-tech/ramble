Support

For problems or suggestions, email hellolink@icloud.com or open an issue on GitHub. It helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (Settings › About).

FAQ

  • Will my data move to a new iPhone? Turn on Settings › iCloud Sync and sign in to the new iPhone with the same Apple Account. You can also export a full backup in Settings › Backup & Export and restore it on the new iPhone.
  • Does AI tidying cost money? Ramble is free, with no in-app purchases. Apple Intelligence and the downloadable open-source models run on your iPhone at no cost. Cloud services such as Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini use your own API key and are billed to you by the provider.
  • How much space do downloaded models take? Each model's size is shown in Settings › AI Tidying and Settings › Speech Recognition. Delete them when you don't need them.
  • A reminder didn't go off? Check that notifications are allowed in the iPhone's Settings › Notifications › Ramble, and that a Focus isn't silencing it.
